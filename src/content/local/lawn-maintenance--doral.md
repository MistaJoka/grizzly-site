Recurring maintenance in Doral is about consistency. Each visit covers mowing, edging along sidewalks, curbs and beds, string trimming around posts and fences, and cleanup of the clippings so the property looks finished when the visit ends.

For associations and property managers, the owner is the person you talk to. Questions about an entrance bed or a one-time cleanup before an event go to the same person who is on the job.
